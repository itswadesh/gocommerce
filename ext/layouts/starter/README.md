# The starting home page's pictures

The starting home page names these, and the engine serves them from its own
binary at `/x/layouts/starter/1/`, to any store without naming one. They are
cached as immutable, and pages shops have saved keep naming them, so a file
here is never changed or removed: a changed picture gets a new file name, and
the old file stays.

Generated on 2026-11-12 with Google Gemini (the owner's Gemini app), in one
conversation so the set shares its light and palette. Each was cropped for
its slot with Pillow, which also drops Gemini's sparkle mark from the
bottom-right corner, and saved as WebP at quality 72–80. None shows a person,
and none stands for any shop's own products: they are a starting point the
shop replaces.

Every prompt asked for the same look: an editorial still life against ivory
limewashed plaster and travertine, soft window daylight from the left, a muted
ivory, sand, clay, olive and brass palette, medium format, a quiet luxury
catalogue look, and ended "No people, no hands, no text, no logos, no
watermark."

1. Hero (16:9): a folded oatmeal handloom linen shirt, a small tan leather
   handbag, a matte clay vase of dried pampas and a brass tray with a gold
   bangle and an amber perfume bottle on a travertine table, the left third
   left calm for words.
2. Hero on phones (3:4): the same scene composed upright, with calm stone
   below for words.
3. Shop all (1:1): a cream woven cotton tote holding folded linen, a ceramic
   cup and a small kraft-paper parcel, on a travertine block.
4. Collections (1:1): three stacks of folded handloom textiles in ivory and
   oatmeal, sand and terracotta, olive and sage, with a small brass bowl.
5. Categories (1:1): a pale oak shelf of four compartments holding leather
   sandals, a clay mug, a brass candle holder and a potted olive plant.
6. Track an order (1:1): a kraft-paper parcel tied with cotton twine and a
   dried sprig, beside a smaller cream box, on a travertine block.
7. Beside the newest products (3:4): a handwoven ivory throw with an olive
   border over a stepped travertine plinth, a tall brass vase, folded linen
   and a dried branch.
8. Lookbook (5:4): an empty, sunlit boutique corner, with folded linen,
   ceramic bowls and a brass tray of jewellery on an oak table by an arched
   window.

### Tiles (Google Gemini)

Generated with Google Gemini (owner's Gemini app), cropped and compressed.
Each crop drops Gemini's sparkle mark in the bottom-right corner. Each prompt
ended "No people, no text, no logos."

1. Shop all: "…1:1 square… a single plain kraft-paper shopping bag with rope
   handles, filled with a few colourful folded items peeking out, standing on
   a pink geometric plinth against a solid bright coral background…"
2. Collections: "…1:1 square… three neat stacks of folded textiles and fabrics
   in coordinated colour families (warm, cool, neutral), each stack on its own
   small geometric plinth, against a solid soft lilac background…"
3. Categories: "…1:1 square… an open modular display shelf made of four
   colourful cubes in a 2x2 grid, each cube holding one different everyday
   product — a white sneaker, a ceramic mug, a pair of headphones, a small
   potted plant — against a solid fresh lime-green background…"
4. Track an order: "…1:1 square… a neatly sealed plain cardboard parcel tied
   with twine, a second smaller parcel stacked on top at an angle, standing on
   a blue geometric plinth against a solid sky-blue background…"

The "…" marks words each shared with the first prompt of the set, which asked
for the same photographic style and lighting.
