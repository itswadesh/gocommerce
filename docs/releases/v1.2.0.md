Additive over v1.1.0: no route, field or exported API was removed or changed in meaning.

## A trade portal for business buyers and dealers (`-b2b -identity`)
- Buyers and dealers sign in at `/portal` on the store's own address with their store account, and see only what their role and company allow (D77).
- The company's terms and what it owes; quick order by SKU, by pasting rows or from a spreadsheet file; the basket, with checkout of only the lines ticked; orders, an order's lines, and repeating one into the open basket; approvals; quotes; statements; the company's catalogue; the team, for company admins; leads, for dealers.
- Pages for accepting a company invitation, resetting a password and confirming an email, so a store without a storefront of its own can run its trade from the engine.

## Selling to businesses (ext/b2b)
- Statements for any period: orders on account, payments, cancellations, a running balance and an aging summary, as JSON or CSV (D74).
- A record of every change to a company's terms (credit limit, net days, approval threshold, PO rule, status, customer group, catalogue) and who made it.
- A quick order can be uploaded as a CSV file; a line that cannot go in names its row.
- A company can be held to a catalogue of chosen categories and products, enforced at checkout, quick order, repeat order, quotes and approvals (D75).

## Shipping and tax per customer group, and so per company
- A shipping rate can name a customer group. That group's buyers are offered its rates in place of the public ones in that zone (D76).
- A customer group can be tax-exempt. Its orders carry no tax and record which group exempted them. In a store whose prices include tax, the price stays the same; give the group a price list to lower it.
- A phone order can choose its delivery option (`shipping_rate_id`).

## Multi-tenant mode
- A console for the platform's operators, on the platform's own hosts: create, rename, suspend, resume and delete stores, attach domains, add owners and replace a store's token (D73).

## Public API
- `GET /api/store`: the shop's name and how to reach it, for a page a buyer sees before signing in.
